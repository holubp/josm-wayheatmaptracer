# 0.22.0-rc.16 — candidate under qualification

This candidate updates **Corridor-aware (A)** tracing in **Precise Shape** mode.
Legacy v0.2-compatible remains the default. Experimental B and the existing
Legacy-then-B workflow retain their RC15 algorithms.

- A can consider up to two additional, source-authorized geometric choices at
  short gaps in its observations. This addresses kinks caused by forcing a noisy
  interpolated point into an otherwise coherent trace. Gap points remain
  unsupported observations, and final image support is measured independently.
- An admitted source-owned bridge keeps its existing geometry. Optional gap
  refinement is skipped for that track, so improving a separate small gap
  cannot remove a bridge that prevents a crossing elsewhere.
- The early A resource check uses separate pair and transition estimates with
  their respective existing limits. Full work, memory, cancellation and final
  geometry checks remain in force.
- A captures have explicit versioned policy and frozen authority. Prior R3
  captures retain their replay behavior. A's Move Existing Nodes mode retains
  its prior policy.
- A diagnostics preserve actual work, resource causes and cancellation ownership.
  Preview, immutable edit planning and Undo/Redo remain subject to validation.

Select **Corridor-aware (A)** in Heatmap settings and use **Precise Shape** to
test these changes. Review the preview before applying it. Sparse competing
strands and ambiguous junctions can still need manual judgment or adjustment.

Stepwise refinement buttons and menu simplification are planned separately in
G004. New B algorithm development is deferred. This RC is an A testing delivery;
the remaining full 0.22.0 release gates are still tracked separately.
