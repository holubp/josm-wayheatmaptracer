# Longitudinal Corridor and Junction Stability Plan

**Status:** Implemented and verified for release `v0.16.0`.

**Post-release correction:** Field testing showed that absence of endpoint-guide evidence and source-scale junction intersections were too strict as hard gates. Release `v0.16.1` keeps missing endpoint evidence as a ranking/diagnostic penalty, restricts connected-way checks to junction-adjacent segments with sampling-aware tolerance, and measures unsupported short excursions against the robust tube. Proven foldbacks, terminal turns, and pre-junction crossings remain blocking.

**Coordinate-contract correction:** The physical-distance, coverage, ranking, final-preview safety, and format-5 diagnostic repair is specified separately in [`2026-08-02-100127-01-plan-corridor-physical-distance-and-ranking-repair.md`](2026-08-02-100127-01-plan-corridor-physical-distance-and-ranking-repair.md).

**Goal:** Make the experimental corridor-aware tracker follow the stable longitudinal center of broad, medium-strength, and sparse heatmap trails without short left/right ripples or temporary strand switches, while giving fixed and movable endpoints explicit branch-approach constraints that prevent terminal kinks, foldbacks, pre-junction crossings, and point cliques.

**Why planning is required:** The observed failures are produced by interacting stages rather than one tunable constant: corridor boundaries are quantized to source samples, association can switch strands during short scale conflicts, the optimizer globally beam-prunes second-order states, its quality metrics do not measure physical longitudinal roughness, and endpoint constraints interpolate offsets without modeling the branch's entry tangent. A local smoothing pass would hide symptoms, could erase real switchbacks, and would violate the one-pass corridor-aware design. The implementation therefore changes evidence representation, association, optimization, endpoint boundary conditions, ranking, safety checks, diagnostics, and tests while preserving the proven legacy path.

**Acceptance:** `TrackerMode.LEGACY_V02` remains the default and is byte-for-byte behaviorally unchanged by corridor-aware code paths. On broad straight, diagonal, and gently curved synthetic corridors, the selected corridor-aware centerline has high-frequency cross-track RMS no greater than `0.15` source pixel, p95 residual no greater than `0.25` source pixel, mean center bias no greater than `0.25` source pixel, and no unsupported one-profile excursion greater than `1.5` source pixels. It retains at least 98% of the pre-change mean sampled intensity and at least 98% shoulder containment. A persistent weak single strand remains traceable, neighboring parallel strands retain separate identities, and a supported sine curve or switchback retains at least 90% of its intended lateral amplitude. For fixed endpoints, the endpoint coordinate remains exact; on a straight synthetic branch the first reliable approach has no foldback, no crossing of the connected way before the junction, and no unsupported local turn above 15 degrees. Movable endpoints remain opt-in and inside their configured displacement bound. Unsafe or unsupported endpoint approaches remain inspectable but cannot be applied. The implementation performs no new tile download, no additional zoom request, no post-hoc geometry averaging, and no hidden internal rerun. Debug bundles expose all new evidence and decisions without credentials. The full Java, Python, fixture, Javadoc, and packaging checks pass before any `v0.16.0` release.

## Measured Baseline

- `problems-3.zip` run `1785623281914` has 369 profiles at approximately `1.553 m/source-pixel`. Its selected geometry has p95 high-frequency residual of about `0.50` source pixel, p95 lateral acceleration of `1.0` source pixel, and 41 offset sign changes, although exported cross-scale persistence and the current acceleration-derived "longitudinal stability" are both close to 1.0.
- Runs `1785623337207` and `1785623375277` reproduce the same rough three-node geometry under different nondeterministic strand labels. They have p95 acceleration near `2.0` source pixels, p95 preview turn near 62 degrees, and a temporary profile-51-to-57 branch switch from roughly `-12 px` to `-84/-60 px` and back while `scaleConflict=true`.
- The same rough runs pin profile 0 to offset zero while the local heatmap core is near `108 px`; the following offsets jump through approximately `36`, `-12`, and `-36 px`. The current endpoint penalty controls scalar offset but does not constrain the actual entry tangent or curvature.
- A diagnostic nine-profile median reduced high-frequency residual and acceleration by about half in the long run while changing mean sampled intensity by only about 1.3%. This is evidence that longitudinal consensus is useful, not approval to add a post-optimization median filter.
- Existing scale persistence measures agreement between blur/downsample levels at one profile. It must not be treated as longitudinal persistence. Existing optimizer stability is an inverse weighted acceleration cost and must not be presented as a physical roughness measurement.

## Required Data Flow

`scalar L0/L1/L2 profiles -> subpixel corridor intervals -> elementary longitudinal track association -> confidence/uncertainty-aware corridor tube -> one constrained second-order optimizer -> physical quality and safety checks -> calibrated ranking -> preview/debug export`

This flow runs independently for each detector mapping and for the existing all-color scalar aggregate. It must not fuse finished geometries. Fine child tracks remain independent when a coarser level merges neighboring paths.

## Non-Goals And Guardrails

- Do not modify legacy peak extraction, legacy no-signal fallback, legacy candidate ranking, or legacy safety behavior.
- Do not add a final moving average, median filter, spline rewrite, repeated optimization, geometry consensus, automatic search widening, parallel-way score, or extra fixed-tile validation pass.
- Do not infer the selected branch's terminal tangent from a connected main road. A side road may legitimately meet at an angle.
- Do not simplify fixed, selected, shared, tagged, or otherwise referenced OSM nodes. Any emergency terminal cleanup may remove only newly generated, untagged, unreferenced interior points.
- Do not commit supplied debug archives, extracted debug contents, calibration bundles, or `exceptions.txt`.

### Outcome 3: Prevent temporary strand switches during association

- Dependencies: Outcome 2.
- Work: Refactor `CorridorTracker` transition scoring to use cumulative physical spacing, source-pixel uncertainty, and a predicted center/slope from the current path state. Calculate prediction residual as `abs(observedOffset - predictedOffset) / sourcePixelSizePx`. A one-profile residual above `1.5` source pixels is not a normal continuation. It may be accepted only when a physically sustained window supports coherent displacement and compatible fine-scale identity; `scaleConflict` and coarse-parent merging can never supply that permission.
- Work: Replace the fixed two-profile gap rule with a bounded physical gap: both elapsed distance at most 20 metres and missing-profile count at most 16. Prefer a gap state over changing to an unrelated side strand. Bridge a gap only when the observations before and after it overlap in shoulder/core identity and predicted position within combined uncertainty. Otherwise terminate or split the candidate and expose the reason; do not invent a center through unsupported space.
- Work: Preserve multiple parallel elementary tracks. Matching uses interval overlap, predicted offset, width compatibility, and gradient/core identity. It must not collapse two fine children merely because a coarse observation covers both. Make candidate identity deterministic by sorting seeds and tie breaks on profile index, continuous center, interval width, and stable evidence keys before assigning a display id.
- Risks/open questions: A real hairpin can move by more than `1.5` source pixels over a short distance. The threshold is a trigger for sustained-motion proof, not an unconditional rejection. Use actual profile spacing and supported turn evidence before accepting it.
- Verify: `sh gradlew test --tests '*CorridorTrackerTest' --tests '*MultiScaleCorridorTrackingTest' --tests '*CorridorGroupingTest' --tests '*CorridorAssignmentServiceTest'`

### Outcome 4: Build a confidence-aware longitudinal corridor tube

- Dependencies: Outcomes 2-3.
- Work: Add focused immutable service records, preferably `CorridorTubeSlice`, `LongitudinalCorridorTube`, and `CorridorTubeBuilder`. A slice contains cumulative physical distance, robust center, core and shoulder intervals, tangent, optional curvature, lateral uncertainty, confidence, scale conflict, parent-merge status, and the contributing raw/B3/B5/fine/coarse observations. Document every field and unit.
- Work: Calculate cumulative distance from profile anchors in the sampled geometry and carry the capture transform needed to express it in metres. Around each profile, use approximately 10 metres of support, clamped to 5-9 profiles. Fit center versus distance with confidence-weighted robust local regression: weighted least squares followed by two Huber reweighting iterations; use `delta = max(0.5 source pixel, 1.5 * weighted MAD)`. Use a local line for center/tangent. Permit a local quadratic curvature reference only when at least 70% of the physical window has compatible, same-direction motion and no scale conflict or coarse-parent merge.
- Work: Start observation weights from signal existence, localization confidence, and cross-scale persistence. Set coarse-center weight to zero for `scaleConflict` or `parentMerge`. Do not let weak absolute signal alone zero a narrow strand that persists longitudinally. For a broad flat core, enlarge lateral uncertainty and strengthen the robust interval-center prior; for a narrow localized strand, reduce center regularization so real weak geometry remains usable.
- Work: Build the tube from the associated elementary identity from Outcome 3. Coarse evidence may tighten uncertainty but may not relocate a fine child to a merged parent midpoint. Store both the robust tube center and the unmodified per-profile evidence so later diagnostics can prove why a point moved.
- Risks/open questions: A fixed 5-9 profile window has different physical span when sample-step settings change, so physical distance selects the window first and the profile clamp only bounds cost. Regression must cover `1`, `6`, and `24` sampled-raster pixels per source pixel.
- Verify: `sh gradlew test --tests '*LongitudinalCorridorTubeTest' --tests '*CorridorScaleInvarianceTest' --tests '*MultiScaleCorridorTrackingTest'`

### Outcome 5: Replace global beam pruning with bounded exact second-order optimization

- Dependencies: Outcomes 2-4.
- Work: Refactor `CorridorCenterlineOptimizer` so each profile has at most 21 deterministic continuous offset states. Mandatory states are fixed/movable endpoint bounds, robust tube center, valid raw/B3/B5 centers, core boundaries, shoulder boundaries, compatible coarse center, and zero where required. Fill remaining capacity with evidence-ranked scalar samples and evenly distributed interval representatives; never discard a mandatory state during reduction.
- Work: Retain the best dynamic-programming path for every `(previousOffset, currentOffset)` pair instead of globally keeping only the cheapest 441 partial paths. Evaluate each next offset, keep deterministic backpointers, and reconstruct once. With at most 21 states this remains bounded to 441 pair states and at most 9261 transitions per profile; do not copy the full offset history into every state.
- Work: Normalize all objective terms. Interpolate scalar evidence at the candidate offset. Keep equivalent intensity values inside the existing scale-disagreement deadband equal. Penalize distance outside the shoulder strongly, distance outside the core moderately, and distance from the robust tube center weakly in proportion to localization uncertainty. Express first-difference continuity as lateral slope over physical profile spacing. Express second-difference curvature from actual candidate geometry relative to the robust longitudinal reference tangent/curvature, not solely relative to the input way.
- Work: Put all corridor-aware weights in one documented immutable `CorridorOptimizationParameters` record with package-private defaults and diagnostics serialization. Start from current data weights (`0.55` core, `4.0` shoulder and the existing `0.02..0.10` plateau deadband) so the first change isolates state precision and longitudinal geometry. Tune only against the acceptance matrix. Do not expose new UI settings in this iteration.
- Work: Do not add a third-order DP state solely for curvature-change. Calculate physical curvature-change after reconstruction as a quality/safety metric. Alternating turns are suppressed by the robust reference and second-order physical curvature term; sustained supported curvature gets the Outcome 4 relaxation. If tests prove second order insufficient, stop and revise this plan rather than silently adding an unbounded optimizer.
- Risks/open questions: Exact pair-state DP can still choose a physically poor path if evidence and objective units are wrong. Every cost row must therefore export unweighted quantities and weighted contributions separately. Fixed constraints must be inserted before state reduction.
- Verify: `sh gradlew test --tests '*CorridorCenterlineOptimizerTest' --tests '*LongitudinalCorridorTubeTest' --tests '*CorridorScaleInvarianceTest' --tests '*CorridorRasterIntegrationTest'`

### Outcome 6: Model endpoint and junction approaches as boundary conditions

- Dependencies: Outcomes 4 and 5.
- Work: Extend `EndpointConstraint` and `JunctionContext`, or add a separate `EndpointApproachEvidence` record if that keeps the records focused. For each fixed-anchor interval, scan inward from each constrained end to find the first reliable interior corridor anchor. A reliable anchor requires selected-track support, acceptable localization or longitudinal persistence, no scale conflict, no parent merge, and no unresolved multimodal crossing. Record why skipped profiles were considered junction-ambiguous.
- Work: Infer the branch approach tangent from the robust corridor tube over a physical baseline, initially 8-15 metres as available, ending at the reliable interior anchor. Ignore the possibly pathological first/last source segment and never force the tangent of another connected way. Construct a cubic-Hermite-equivalent endpoint guide from the exact endpoint to the interior anchor, using the branch tangent at the interior side and the least-curved feasible terminal tangent at the endpoint. Use this guide only as a position/tangent/curvature term inside the one optimizer from Outcome 5.
- Work: Taper direct heatmap-center weight toward the endpoint when profiles are multimodal, merged, scale-conflicted, or intersect a strong crossing corridor. Keep shoulder-exit cost and topology safety active. For a fixed endpoint, allow only exact offset zero. For an opt-in movable endpoint, retain the original coordinate as a strong prior and enforce the existing hard maximum displacement; movement must improve valid local heatmap evidence and cannot be justified solely by the newly traced branch.
- Work: If no reliable interior anchor supports a smooth approach, keep the endpoint and mark that candidate `UNSUPPORTED_ENDPOINT_APPROACH`; it may be previewed but not applied. A bounded fallback may connect the last sane optimized point directly to the endpoint only when the segment has forward progress and creates no crossing or foldback. It may prune only newly generated untagged/unreferenced interior points inside that terminal interval and must preserve fixed, selected, shared, tagged, and referenced nodes.
- Risks/open questions: A real road may bend sharply immediately before a junction.
