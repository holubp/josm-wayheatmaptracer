# v0.18.0 Sparse Longitudinal Corridor Inference

**Status:** Implemented and under release verification from maintainer-validated `v0.17.1`.

**Goal:** Release `v0.18.0` with a corridor-aware hierarchy that combines intermittent, longitudinally coherent heatmap strands into an explicit sparse-corridor parent, centers that parent robustly through cross-sectional holes, suppresses weak-corridor kinks and ripples, preserves supported turns, and retains elementary child candidates when one-versus-multiple-way interpretation is uncertain.

**Why planning is required:** This is an architectural extension of experimental corridor-aware inference. `v0.17.0` accurately samples source pixels and substantially improves center stability, but it still extracts and optimizes one elementary strand at a time. In low-activity corridors, independent cross-sections contain disconnected peaks and internal zeros; profile-local shoulders and fixed valley thresholds fragment one physical corridor into incomplete tracks. A safe correction must add longitudinal bundle semantics without blurring parallel roads together, hiding child alternatives, flattening switchbacks, changing legacy behavior, or making post-hoc geometry consensus.

**Acceptance:** The implementation runs only in `TrackerMode.CORRIDOR_AWARE`; `LEGACY_V02` remains byte-for-byte behaviorally unchanged and default. Raw/B3/B5/multi-scale scalar evidence and elementary `CorridorTrack` candidates remain available. After elementary longitudinal association, a new bundle stage may create parent candidates from two or more compatible tracks using physical-distance, tangent, persistence, occupancy, separation, and valley evidence. Cross-sectional zero is treated as weak or missing evidence when local activity is sparse, and as separation evidence only when two modes are independently persistent with stable order and a longitudinally persistent valley. Bundle coverage uses the union of direct child evidence plus explicitly bounded interpolation; it never labels a long unsupported invention complete. Bundle centers use robust longitudinally predicted child positions, uncertainty, and actual scalar intensity evidence before the existing exact second-order optimizer. Weak/broad/ambiguous evidence increases pre-optimizer stability regularization, while a turn relaxes it only after sustained raw/B3/B5 and longitudinal support. No broad lateral blur, completed-geometry averaging, free moving average, hidden rerun, or automatic OSM lane semantics is introduced. Parent and child candidates remain selectable and rateable. Dense, medium, sparse, holey, outlier, sustained-turn, switchback, same-road multi-strand, and true-parallel-road regressions pass. On `last-slide-debug-1785794101840.zip`, native hot-family bundle evidence is longitudinally complete where child-union evidence supports it, intermittent holes do not cause strand resets, and non-sustained high-frequency p95 is targeted at no more than `0.40` source pixel without reducing supported turn/switchback amplitude below 90%. Full tests, fixture archive, analyzers, Javadoc, performance bounds, debug redaction, packaging, and manifest checks pass before explicitly authorized publication.

## Confirmed Evidence

- The post-release bundle contains 242 profiles over approximately `625.5 m`, with median profile spacing around `2.60 m` and native z15 source-pixel resolution around `1.55 m`.
- Native `hot` contains multiple disconnected elementary bands in 111 profiles but profile-local parents in only 11. `hot-corridor` contains multiple bands in 122 profiles but parents in only 19. `hot-strict` is 91 versus 6; `all-colors-combined` is 120 versus 28.
- `hot-corridor` produces one `0.897`-support strand and three fragments with support around `0.236-0.256`; no longitudinal parent track represents their union. Forty-one of 50 total candidates are incomplete under strand-specific coverage.
- The highest-ranked `hot-corridor` child has non-sustained high-frequency p95 around `0.502` source pixel and eight unsupported reversals. It remains below the current `0.60` blocking threshold, demonstrating that the safety limit is not a sufficient quality objective.
- Existing synthetic coverage tests model a continuously filled broad corridor, a single sparse strand with short missing profiles, or two persistent modes with a uniformly shallow/deep valley. They do not model several intermittent traces, internal zero holes, changing lateral order, or child-union coverage.
- The archive contains no human rating or paired manually traced ground truth. It proves structural fragmentation and measurable ripple, but final absolute center-bias calibration should use an added private human estimate or an equivalent rated bundle without committing private artifacts.

## Fixed Design Decisions

- Preserve every `v0.17.x` coordinate, source-pixel, convolution-support, exact-optimizer, endpoint, node-identity, undo/redo, topology-safety, and debug-redaction contract.
- Run color-to-intensity conversion before all corridor operations. Bundle logic consumes scalar profiles and must be independent of palette name except for existing source-tier/ranking metadata.
- Preserve raw, B3 `[1,2,1]`, B5 `[1,4,6,4,1]`, fine half-phase, and Gaussian-scale evidence. Do not fill holes by broad lateral convolution or synthesize lower zoom imagery.
- Keep elementary child tracks and their unmodified candidates. A sparse bundle is an additional parent hypothesis, not a replacement and not post-hoc geometry consensus.
- Treat activity as a continuum. Do not add hard `low`, `medium`, and `high` detector modes. The evidential meaning of a valley depends continuously on joint modal persistence and local occupancy.
- Build bundle geometry before optimization. The existing exact bounded second-order pair-state dynamic program remains the only geometry optimizer.
- Use OSM parallel-way context only under the existing opt-in `parallelWayAwareness` setting and only as read-only ranking evidence. It cannot prove lane or carriageway semantics and cannot move contextual primitives.
- Keep incomplete and unsafe candidates visible for inspection/rating but not applicable. Do not lower safety gates merely to make a new parent usable.
- Keep every requested detector run. Performance improvements may cache shared evidence and deduplicate equivalent hypotheses, but may not silently omit a mapping or child candidate.

### Outcome 2: Represent sparse bundle evidence without changing elementary tracks

- Work: Add explicit immutable model types owned by the corridor-aware service, such as `SparseCorridorBundle` and `SparseCorridorBundlePoint`. A bundle records its deterministic id, child track ids, classification (`combined`, `ambiguous`, or `separate` decision evidence), profile-aligned direct and predicted contributors, robust center, core/shoulder envelope, uncertainty, union support, occupancy, valley persistence, tangent agreement, and grouping reasons.
- Work: Keep `CorridorTrack` and `CorridorTrackPoint` elementary semantics intact. Convert a supported bundle to an explicit parent `CorridorTrack` only at the adapter boundary used by `CorridorTubeBuilder` and `CorridorCenterlineOptimizer`; retain side metadata so direct evidence is never confused with prediction.
- Work: Distinguish at least `DIRECT_UNION` bundle points from `BOUNDED_INTERPOLATION`. Direct-union means one or more child tracks are observed at that profile. Bounded interpolation means compatible evidence exists on both longitudinal sides within the existing maximum 16 profiles and 20 metres. Predicted-only points never count as direct support.
- Work: Validate every model value: ordered intervals, finite center/uncertainty, known child ids, monotonic profile indexes, and no contributor outside the bundle. Make collections immutable and document classes, methods, parameters, and units.

### Outcome 3: Replace valley-only pair grouping with longitudinal bundle hypotheses

- Work: Rework `CorridorGrouping` or introduce a narrowly owned `SparseCorridorBundleBuilder` after `CorridorTracker`. Compute pair evidence over physical windows centered on observed profiles, initially using the existing 12 m stability half-window and never raster/profile-count approximations for metres.
- Work: For each pair, fit robust local lateral predictions against cumulative metres. Record joint and union support, p90 tangent difference, robust lateral separation and variation, lateral-order stability, common-envelope support, valley ratio distribution, occupancy, and direct-versus-interpolated contribution.
- Work: Treat a pair as strong separation evidence only when both tracks independently cover at least 70% of a common span of at least 20 m, lateral order is stable in at least 90% of joint observations, a deep valley (`<=0.40` of the weaker local peak) persists in at least 60% of joint observations, and separation exceeds combined uncertainty with an initial floor of 1.5 source pixels. Keep these starting values named, documented, and adjustable in package-private parameters for tests rather than UI settings.
- Work: Permit a combined sparse hypothesis when union support is at least 70%, p90 tangent difference is at most 20 degrees, separation remains inside the configured search envelope, and the tracks are complementary/intermittent or the deep valley lacks persistent jointly observed modal support. Mark the middle evidence region ambiguous and retain parent and children.
- Work: Build multi-track groups from compatible pair evidence, but prevent transitive chain merging: every accepted group must pass all-pairs tangent/proximity checks and a maximum robust envelope check. Split a component when a distant `A-C` pair is incompatible even if `A-B` and `B-C` are compatible.
- Work: Keep current continuously filled shallow-valley parent behavior as a regression-compatible subset. Do not make literal zero itself positive evidence; it merely carries little separation weight when joint modal persistence is weak.
- Risks/open questions: Multi-lane versus divided-road interpretation can be intrinsically ambiguous from heatmap pixels. In that case the correct output is selectable parent and child hypotheses, not a forced semantic decision.
- Verify: `sh gradlew test --tests '*CorridorGroupingTest' --tests '*CorridorRasterIntegrationTest' --tests '*MultiScaleCorridorTrackingTest'`

### Outcome 4: Estimate a robust longitudinal bundle center and uncertainty

- Work: At each target profile, gather direct child centers plus child positions predicted by robust local fits inside the 12 m half-window. Reject predictions requiring more than the existing 20 m gap, incompatible tangent, a scale-conflict strand switch, or extrapolation without evidence on both sides unless it is an endpoint approach handled separately.
- Work: Weight contributors by signal-existence confidence, localization confidence, scale persistence, longitudinal persistence, and distance from the target profile. Winsorize a transient contributor's raw weight relative to the local contributor-weight distribution so one isolated bright strand cannot dominate a persistent bundle.
- Work: Compute the bundle center with a deterministic robust Huber location initialized from the weighted envelope midpoint. Use at least two reweighting iterations and a scale floor of `0.5` source pixel. For symmetric sparse children the result must be the midpoint rather than whichever child happens to sort first.
- Work: Compute uncertainty from the source-pixel floor, weighted residual/MAD, child localization uncertainty, contributor count, and direct-versus-predicted support. Inflate uncertainty for one-contributor and interpolation-only profiles; do not increase confidence merely because a synthetic center lies between unsupported traces.
- Work: Build a synthetic parent band retaining the union shoulder/core envelope and contributor centers. Actual raw/B3/B5 scalar intensity remains available to the optimizer; the bundle center is a longitudinal prior, not fabricated pixel intensity.
- Work: Preserve the existing high-intensity-core behavior for a single broad connected corridor. Sparse-bundle logic must not recenter a strong native core on its low-threshold outer shoulders.
- Verify: `sh gradlew test --tests '*CorridorGroupingTest' --tests '*LongitudinalCorridorTubeTest' --tests '*CorridorRasterIntegrationTest'`

## Execution Order And Stop Conditions

1. Do not implement or publish this plan from an unvalidated `v0.17.1`. If the junction patch requires `v0.17.2`, update this plan's base before corridor work proceeds.
2. Outcomes 1-3 must prove sparse grouping and true-parallel separation before any optimizer weight changes. Do not tune smoothing to compensate for incorrect bundle membership.
3. Outcomes 4-5 must produce explicit uncertainty and honest union coverage before a bundle can become applicable.
4. Outcome 6 may change only the corridor-aware pre-optimizer evidence and existing exact objective. Stop if exact state bounds, deterministic tie behavior, or supported-turn retention cannot be preserved.
5. Stop if a low-activity hole causes the parent to jump to zero/source offset, if a short outlier pulls the parent, or if a predicted-only corridor becomes complete without bounded evidence on both sides.
6. Stop if two independently persistent parallel corridors are silently collapsed, if child candidates disappear, or if OSM context becomes a hard semantic decision.
7. Stop if any fixed/shared/tagged node protection, proposed-junction safety, undo/redo behavior, downloaded-area validation, tile request, palette mapping, aggregate intensity, or legacy behavior changes.
8. Stop publication if real-bundle improvement depends on a palette-specific geometry hack, private fixture committed to Git, unbounded runtime growth, or suppression of a genuine safety warning.

## Handoff Notes For Lower-Cost Implementers

- Implement one outcome at a time in red-green order and rerun its focused suite before continuing.
- Keep units in identifiers: `distanceMeters`, `offsetPx`, `sourcePixel`, `uncertaintyPx`, and `angleDegrees`.
- Do not represent an unobserved bundle point as direct heatmap evidence. Preserve direct, interpolated, and unsupported states through coverage and diagnostics.
- Do not make a sparse parent by averaging completed child geometries. Group observations/tracks first, build one parent evidence field, and run the existing optimizer once on that parent.
- Do not globally raise smoothing. Improve bundle membership and center evidence first; then let uncertainty control the existing objective.
- Preserve the user's untracked `exceptions.txt` and `human-guesstimate*.osm`, and never commit supplied debug/calibration archives.
