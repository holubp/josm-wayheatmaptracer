# v0.20.3 Longest Non-Branching Segment Execution

## Baseline And Scope

- Baseline: public `v0.20.2`, commit `ac6b7c0`.
- Target version: `0.20.3`, explicitly selected by the maintainer.
- Scope: `Select Longest Heatmap Segment`, maximal junction-bounded selection,
  repeated-node eligibility, resolver compatibility, focused tests, and docs.
- Excluded: tracing, tiles, palettes, sampling, cleanup, preview, candidate
  ranking, apply commands, and undo/redo behavior.
- Mutation boundary: the helper may replace the JOSM selection only. It creates
  no command and does not modify any OSM primitive.

## Behavioral Contract

1. One selected way chooses the longest eligible maximal range between way
   endpoints or nodes shared by another way.
2. One selected way plus one uniquely occurring node chooses the longest such
   range containing the node inclusively.
3. A selected junction belongs to both adjacent ranges. The longer eligible
   range wins; exact ties retain the earlier range in way order.
4. Repeated-node-ambiguous maximal ranges are skipped, never shortened at
   arbitrary cut points.
5. Successful output is the selected way plus the chosen two endpoints and is
   immediately accepted by the unchanged `SelectionResolver`.
6. Extra selected nodes, ways, relations, off-way hints, repeated hints, and
   ways without an eligible range fail before selection mutation.
