# v0.21.5 Reviewable Narrow Corridors

**Goal:** Restore practical 7-10 m corridor alignment and replace dead-end inspection-only results with a safe, explicit review-and-apply workflow.

**Why planning is required:** Candidate applicability, modeless preview identity, topology safety, and JOSM data mutation form one safety boundary; the release also changes public UI and diagnostics and will be published externally.

**Target:** `v0.21.5`, based on `10e5ac8` (`0.21.4`). Preserve the unrelated untracked `exceptions.txt`, `human-guesstimate.osm`, and `human-guesstimate2.osm`.

**Acceptance:** At 7.01 m, clipped evidence never becomes a measured center; candidates with meaningful signal whose only deficiency is incomplete longitudinal evidence are shown with their exact final preview and can be explicitly confirmed; confirmation never overrides no-signal, geometry, assignment, topology, stale-source, or downloaded-area failures; Apply consumes exactly the reviewed immutable geometry and assignments; confirmation is session-local and invalidated by candidate or source changes; shared junction constraints remain automatic, contextual OSM ways remain read-only and opt-in, and the proven corridor-aware and legacy v0.2 association algorithms are unchanged; diagnostics identify automatic, review-required, confirmed, and blocked outcomes; version, documentation, tag, release, and jar consistently identify `0.21.5`.

### Outcome 1: Preserve proven narrow-window association safety

- Work: Test the proposed candidate-owned censored-gap relaxation against focused synthetic branch/sustained-bend cases and the private sparse-corridor replay. The private replay proved that the relaxation changed child-strand membership, removed the stable sparse-parent classification, and increased high-frequency ripple from `0.303` to `0.618` source pixels. Remove the relaxation rather than weakening a proven association safety boundary.

Reuse loaded shared-node junction constraints only as bounded selected-branch evidence; contextual OSM ways remain read-only and opt-in. Do not modify the legacy tracker.
