# v0.21.1 Narrow Search Reliability

**Goal:** Restore reliable corridor extraction and stable geometry at 7-14 m search half-widths while preserving true search-edge censoring, switchbacks, legacy tracking, and the pending empty-Strava-tile display correction.

**Why planning is required:** This patch changes corridor evidence semantics, run-scoped retry limits, optional nearby-way ranking, managed imagery display behavior, and public release state. A mistaken boundary rule can authorize off-corridor geometry or suppress real turns, and publication is consequential external state.

**Target:** `v0.21.1`, based on public `v0.21.0` (`7af9a6a`). The untracked `exceptions.txt`, `human-guesstimate.osm`, and `human-guesstimate2.osm` remain outside the mutation and release boundary.

**Acceptance:** A fully bracketed heatmap core remains measured at 7.01, 10, and 14 m half-width even when the native source-pixel pitch is large; a core that actually reaches or leaves the decision boundary remains censored and inspection-only as appropriate; outside-window pixels cannot influence profile filtering, bands, or candidates; ordinary wider-search retry never proposes more than 14 m and is unavailable at or above that bound; changing search width alone does not weaken nearby-way displacement penalties; narrow-window switchbacks do not select adjacent limbs; the managed JOSM layer renders confirmed spatial 404/no-tile responses as transparent while alignment and probe semantics remain unchanged; version, documentation, manifest, tag, release body, and jar asset consistently identify `0.21.1`; all focused and full verification passes before commit, push, tag, or release.
