# Reviewed native admission guarantee

Status: independent Sol scope review accepted; clarifies the approved design,
optional-emission amendment and native-integral-v2 section4. No safety gate waived.

Admission certifies uniquely measured native-mode continuation at the declared
resolution, exact continuous scalar support on the emitted segment, and whole-
segment decision/acquisition authority. It does not certify continuous critical-
component topology, semantic branch identity or absence of every sub-probe split.

The approved amendment requires actual connector geometry, scalar support, branch
evidence and original authority. Section4 specifies complete unique compatible
transverse modes at no more than half-native-pitch spacing, unique mutual measured
continuation within1.5native pixels, and exact bilinear minima inside the measured
support envelope. It explicitly describes local geometric scalar-branch evidence,
not semantic carriageway ownership.

The later draft requirement for a globally disk-shaped shoulder component,
Euler characteristic, manifold links or continuous critical-maximum identity was
an additional theorem. It is not imposed by the approved scope. An off-connector
hole can defeat disk topology without making the actual connector unsupported;
minor attraction toward finite bright side spurs was also deferred by the user.
The CAD/disk design remains an unimplemented research proposal, not an adoption
or release prerequisite. Remove it from the implementation critical path.

Still mandatory: ordered endpoint-to-branch ownership; measured center/sigma/flank
checks; uniquely compatible complete modes and unique mutual continuation over all
relevant measured alternatives; rejection of detected split/merge or ambiguous
ownership; original source observed/gap/censored classifications; exact continuous
scalar-envelope and full authority checks; strict valid-cell/supercover support;
shared resource/cancellation controls; and final-preview geometry/topology checks.

Missing, censored, suppressed-only, competing or nonuniquely continued native modes
remain unavailable. A scalar minimum or bright mean cannot substitute for those
predicates. Do not credit consumed source observations with fabricated positions,
offsets or direct support, and never claim finite probes prove an untested theorem.

Required adversaries include bright endpoints with an interior bilinear dip,
competing ports/parallel-strand exchange, measured split/merge, missing endpoint
binding, invalid interior/corner cells and a narrow authority hole. Off-connector
holes/allowed spurs must not create a new disk veto when the actual declared
predicates pass. Preserve broad measured plateaus, opposite arcs, supported sharp
switchbacks and strong/weak strands. A sub-probe adversary's finite-predicate result
must explicitly leave continuous critical topology unproved.

This correction removes an implementation-added requirement; it does not establish
R4 admission, full graph practicality, repaired engine quality or release readiness.
Those still require production execution and the full original plan's tests.
