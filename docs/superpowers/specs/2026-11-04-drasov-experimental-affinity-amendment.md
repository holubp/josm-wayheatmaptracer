## Ordinary edge classification

Publish COMPATIBLE, INCOMPATIBLE or UNKNOWN independently of native evaluator
status. COMPATIBLE needs source-owned endpoint and actual-connector continuation
evidence. INCOMPATIBLE needs the positive crosscut proof below. Missing support,
NO_COMPLETE_MODE, joins, ambiguous association and unsupported proof geometry are
UNKNOWN. Work, memory, family and cancellation failures remain terminal failures,
never UNKNOWN or a truncated inventory.

A proved incompatible edge is absent from every applicable recurrence stage and
final geometry certification; a large finite cost is not equivalent. UNKNOWN
retains old ordinary eligibility without fabricated coverage or cost/ranking bonus.
Selected UNKNOWN edges explicitly make affinity certification incomplete. No new
public Apply gate is authorized by this experimental amendment.

## Positive crosscut proof

Freeze an injective source-slab chart, transverse coordinates and boundary order.
A verified affine parallelogram with parallel frozen normals and nonzero source
displacement is a sufficient first version. Unsupported charts give UNKNOWN;
do not infer parallelism from an epsilon. This special case alone cannot qualify
curved Drasov geometry.

At deterministic stations bounded by factual half-native physical pitch, preserve
complete accepted modes and unique forward/backward direct links for both endpoint
corridors. Include relevant alternatives. Censored/suppressed/ambiguous competing
evidence cannot establish a unique two-chain proof. Actual center connectors need
owned scalar support and recorded thresholds; brightness alone is insufficient.

Construct the deterministic midpoint crosscut, simple and ordered in the chart,
strictly between the two core-plus-uncertainty supports and spanning both slab
boundaries. Enumerate the closed-cell supercover of every piece, including grid
edge/vertex contacts. Pieces share covered endpoints. Every contributing cell's
nodes must be valid and strictly below the minimum of the four endpoint shoulder
thresholds for that piece. Retain repeated-cell threshold ownership. Failed order,
coverage or strict threshold proof gives UNKNOWN; do not search for another ribbon.

Each classified edge must independently bind its exact endpoint owners to opposite
chains and cross the proved ribbon interior over positive physical length. Mere
tangency is insufficient. Check actual connector acquisition and source authority.
This proves only the declared source-resolution incompatibility, not road semantics.
